# Privacy policy

[Español](PRIVACY.es.md) · **English**

*Last updated: 2026-11-11 · Applies to AutoAlToque (`io.github.matcarfer.autoaltoque`), all versions.*

**AutoAlToque does not collect, share or sell any data. Nothing leaves your phone.**

- **No servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.** The app contains no third-party libraries.
- **On-device only:** cars, logbooks, fault history, adapters and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are erased with *Options → Factory reset* or by uninstalling the app. Android's own backup may copy app data to your Google account if you have it enabled; that is controlled by Android, not by the app.
- **Bluetooth:** used only to connect to your OBD2 adapter. On Android 11 and older, Android requires the *location* permission to scan for Bluetooth devices; the app never reads your location.
- **Notifications:** only alerts about your car and logbook due dates, generated on the phone.
- **Internet:** the app doesn't even have Android's internet permission. Links (searching a fault code on Google/Gemini/ChatGPT, supporting the project, GitHub) open in your browser only when you tap them, and then that site's privacy policy applies. A fault-code search includes the car data shown on screen so you can review it before sending.
- **Exports:** CSV exports and shares happen only when you choose, to the place you choose.
- **Children:** the app is not directed to children and collects nothing from anyone.

Questions: open an [issue](https://github.com/matcarfer/AutoAlToque/issues/new/choose). If this policy ever changes, the change will be listed in the [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md).
